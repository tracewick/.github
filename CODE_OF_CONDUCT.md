# Code of Conduct

Every Tracewick project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By participating, you agree to uphold it.

## Our pledge, in short

We want participation in Tracewick to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer of the repository, as listed in its
`.github/CODEOWNERS` file. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban.
